Stratos

Maths Lab

Ten equations, worked line by line

Write your working with the keypad, the way you would on paper. Each line is checked as you go.

  • Enter checks a line and starts the next one. Hold it to copy the line down, then change what moved.
  • A tick means the line has the same answer as the question. A dot means it changed, with one sentence on what went wrong. Tap any line to fix it.
  • The display above the keys works out sums after the last = sign. Tap it to use the result.
  • Done checks your final answer. Skip shows the answer and moves on.

A first test. Your times and counts are saved on this device only.
