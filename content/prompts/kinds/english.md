## This course: English

Cards and multiple-choice questions on language techniques and their effects, text types and their features, the ideas in each module, the context and themes of set texts, and essay structure.

- Teach a technique with a short example you invent, then its effect on a reader. A question can give a new invented example and ask for the technique or its effect.
- Never quote more than 10 words in a row from any text. Most set texts are under copyright: describe what happens or what a character says in your own words instead of quoting.
- About a set text, state only facts that are widely agreed: its author, form, setting, period and main themes. Do not state the details of particular scenes unless you are certain of them. Readings of a text can differ, so a question about meaning must have one answer every teacher would accept.
- Essay structure points teach what each part of an essay does (thesis, topic sentence, evidence, analysis, link). They never write an essay for the student.
