## This course: Maths

Cards and multiple-choice questions for now. Working on the keypad comes later.

- A card teaches a method in plain words with one short worked example.
- Questions have number answers or name a step. The wrong options come from real mistakes: a sign error, the wrong order of operations, a step skipped, a unit mixed up.
- Work out every answer twice, by two different ways when you can, before you write it. A wrong answer in maths is never acceptable.

## Writing maths

Write every piece of maths in LaTeX between \( and \), inside the sentence: "The axis of symmetry is \(x = -\frac{b}{2a}\)." The app draws it as real maths and reads it aloud to screen readers. Never mark maths with dollar signs: $ is for money, as in $20. Inside maths, money is \$20.

- Put in maths every equation, expression, inequality, variable and number with a sign: "If \(a > 0\)", "the value of \(x\)", "\(y = -3\)", "the point \((3, -4)\)", "an answer of \(-8\)". A plain number with no sign can stay as a word: "4 units from 3", "radius 7".
- Fractions: \frac{a}{b}, with both parts in braces. Never a slash: write \(x = -\frac{b}{2a}\), never x = -b/2a, which reads as (-b/2)a.
- Powers: x^{2}, e^{3x}, 2^{x + 1}, always in braces. Never ² or a caret outside maths.
- Roots: \sqrt{x}, \sqrt{b^{2} - 4ac}, \sqrt[3]{8}.
- Signs: \times, \div, \pm, \le, \ge, \ne, \approx. A minus sign is a plain - inside maths: the app draws it as a real minus sign. Never write − or a hyphen as a minus outside maths.
- Greek letters and constants: \pi, \theta, \Delta, \infty. Degrees: 30^{\circ}.
- Logarithms and functions: \log_{e} x, \ln x, \sin x, \cos 2x, \tan \theta, f(x).
- Combinations: the syllabus's notation for the course. NSW writes {}^{n}C_{r}; Victoria and the Australian Curriculum write \binom{n}{r}.
- Limits, sums and integrals: \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}, \sum_{k=1}^{n} k, \int_{0}^{1} x^{2} \, dx.
- Absolute value: |x - 3|. Brackets: ( ) and [ ], and \{ \} for a set. Write every bracket the maths needs, so it means one thing only.
- Words inside maths: \text{cm}. Keep units such as cm, km/h and $ outside maths where you can: "a length of 12 cm".
- Spaces inside maths change nothing, so a number such as 100 000 is written 100{,}000 inside maths, or kept as a word outside it.
- Use only these commands: frac, sqrt, binom, times, div, pm, cdot, le, ge, ne, approx, pi, theta and other Greek letters, infty, to, log, ln, sin, cos, tan, lim, int, sum, text, left, right, circ, overline, mid, cup, cap, in.
- Keep each option short as it looks on screen: \(\frac{12}{5}\) counts as two characters, not twelve.
- A title is words only, with no maths.
- In JSON every backslash is written twice: "\\(x = -\\frac{b}{2a}\\)".

## Terms

In Mathematics Advanced, Extension 1 and 2, Mathematical Methods and Specialist Mathematics, use the syllabus's terms, never everyday words for them: concave up and concave down, not opens up or a U shape; turning point and stationary point, not a hill, a valley or where it turns back; vertex, not corner; point of inflection, not where it flattens.
