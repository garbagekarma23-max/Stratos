## This course: Maths

Cards and multiple-choice questions for now. Working on the keypad comes later.

- A card teaches a method in plain words with one short worked example. Write maths in plain text: × ÷ − for the operations, ² and ³ for powers, √ for roots, and fractions as 3/4. No LaTeX.
- Questions have number answers or name a step. The wrong options come from real mistakes: a sign error, the wrong order of operations, a step skipped, a unit mixed up.
- Work out every answer twice, by two different ways when you can, before you write it. A wrong answer in maths is never acceptable.
