# Points that would be better with a graph

A census of every point in the banks, for planning the sessions that add graphs (`src/06-figure.js`, `content/prompts/figures.md`). Each point was read in full, card and questions, by a separate classifier in November 2026, and counted only when a teacher would naturally draw a graph for that exact idea and a student understands it better by seeing it. A point that only mentions a graph, a definition, a calculation a graph adds nothing to, or anything that needs a picture or diagram rather than a plot on axes (a cell, a map, a circuit, a timeline) does not count. It is a judgement, so treat it as a plan, not a rule.

"Now" means the graph format can draw it. Seventeen need a chart kind it does not have yet: a bar or column chart, a histogram, a box plot or a slope field.

A shared point is listed under every course that uses it, and counted once in its bank.

The "Have one already" columns and the "Has one already" notes are updated as each session adds graphs. Session 1 added them to Mathematical Methods Units 1 to 4 and Mathematics Advanced Year 11. Session 2 added them to Mathematics Advanced Year 12, Specialist Mathematics Units 1 to 4 and General Mathematics Units 1 and 2.
